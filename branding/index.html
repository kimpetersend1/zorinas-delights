06 / Brand care

Keep it beautifully simple.

A few practical guidelines to help keep the identity consistent across future applications.

01

Give it room

Leave clear space around the wordmark. Use the compact lockup or monogram when the full logo becomes too small.

02

Keep the colours

Use the cocoa version on light surfaces and the reverse wordmark on dark surfaces. Avoid stretching or recolouring the mark.

03

Keep marks separate

The owner-supplied Halaal emblem is a separate asset. Confirm permission to use it and the flyer imagery; do not imply independent certification.
